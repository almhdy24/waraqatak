# Release notes — 1.1.3 (build 7)

Draft "What's new" text for the Google Play listing. It deliberately avoids
mentioning payment, keys or pricing. Play limits the field to 500
characters per language.

```
• Complete business UI/UX overhaul with categorized reports and analytics.
• Perpetual moving weighted-average inventory costing and valuation.
• Physical stocktakes, bulk price updates, and customer account statements.
• Product catalog CSV import/export and RTL alignment refinements.
```
